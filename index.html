Who we are

A small effort focused on practical support.

Water Search & Recovery Help began with a focus on helping families and law enforcement connect with additional resources for water-related search and recovery efforts. Our role is to help people understand possible options and make connections when appropriate.

♡

Family-centered support

We aim to communicate respectfully, listen carefully, and treat requests and family circumstances with discretion.

⌁

Resource connections

Depending on the situation, possible resources may include qualified dive teams, sonar operators, water search K9 teams, drone pilots, or remotely operated vehicles (ROVs).

▤

Public education

We share general information about search methods, coordination, safety, and the limits of what anyone can promise.

How we help

A careful, case-by-case process.

Every situation is different. We do not assume that one resource or search method is right for every case.

Contact us privately

Tell us briefly what kind of help you are looking for and how we can reach you. Please do not send sensitive documents or detailed personal information in an initial request.

Understand the situation

We may ask a few follow-up questions to understand the type of resources being requested and whether the appropriate agency is aware.

Explore possible connections

Where appropriate, we may help identify potential resources for the family or coordinating agency to consider. Availability, suitability, travel, and costs vary.

Important: If law enforcement or another agency is coordinating the case, we encourage families to keep that agency informed. We do not direct official searches or replace the incident command structure.
Our approach

Respectful, discreet, and realistic.

Families deserve support that does not add pressure or create false expectations. We aim to keep communication private and to be honest about what we can and cannot do.

  • We do not promise that a person will be found or that a search will have a particular outcome.
  • We encourage coordination with law enforcement and the agency responsible for the case.
  • We do not publish private family details or case information without appropriate permission.
  • We recognize that search and recovery work can be dangerous and requires appropriate training, equipment, and coordination.

What we are — and what we are not

We are an independent resource-connection effort. We are not a law enforcement agency, emergency dispatch service, or a substitute for professional search-and-rescue coordination. We do not represent that every listed or contacted resource is available, endorsed, or suitable for every incident.

Contact us privately

To receive requests, connect this section to a Google Form that you control before publishing the site. Keep the form short and collect only information you need to respond.

  1. Ask for a first name or preferred way to address the person.
  2. Ask for a safe contact method and general location.
  3. Ask what kind of assistance or resources they are seeking.
  4. Ask whether law enforcement or the coordinating agency is aware.
